# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 8 November 2026_

TabTrack does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

## What is stored

To save and restore sessions, TabTrack stores the following **only on your
device**, in Chrome's local extension storage:

- The URLs and titles of tabs in sessions you save
- Pinned state and tab-group names/colours of those tabs
- An automatic snapshot of your open (non-incognito) windows, used to offer
  recovery after the browser closes or crashes
- Your TabTrack settings

Nothing is sent to any server. TabTrack contains no analytics, tracking or
remote code. Uninstalling the extension deletes all of this data.

Exported files are created only when you click **Export Sessions**, and are
saved wherever you choose.

## Permissions

| Permission  | Why it is needed                                        |
|-------------|---------------------------------------------------------|
| `tabs`      | Read the URLs and titles of tabs you choose to save     |
| `tabGroups` | Save and restore tab-group names and colours            |
| `storage`   | Keep your sessions and settings on your device          |
| `alarms`    | Schedule the periodic auto-save used for crash recovery |
