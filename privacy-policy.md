# TabTrack - Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 9 November 2026 (version 1.3.0)

## Overview

TabTrack is a Chrome extension that lets you save, restore and manage your browser
tab sessions. TabTrack does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

## Data stored on your device

TabTrack stores the following **only on your device**, using Chrome's local
extension storage:

- Names of saved sessions (as provided by you)
- URLs and titles of the tabs in your saved sessions
- Pinned state and tab-group names/colours of those tabs
- Timestamps of when sessions were saved or updated
- An automatic snapshot of your open (non-incognito) windows, used only to offer
  recovery after the browser closes or crashes
- Your TabTrack settings

TabTrack does not transmit any data to external servers and does not share any
data with third parties. It contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or
remotely hosted code.

Exported files are created only when you click **Export Sessions**, and are saved
wherever you choose.

## Permissions used

| Permission  | Why it is needed                                                      |
|-------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------|
| `tabs`      | Read the URLs and titles of your open tabs when you save a session    |
| `tabGroups` | Save and restore tab-group names and colours                          |
| `storage`   | Store your sessions and settings locally on your device               |
| `alarms`    | Schedule the periodic local auto-save used for crash recovery         |

## Data security

All data is kept in your browser's local extension storage, protected by your
browser's own security mechanisms.

## Data retention

Saved sessions remain on your device until you delete them in TabTrack or
uninstall the extension. Uninstalling removes all TabTrack data.
